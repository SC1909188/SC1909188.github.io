Mini lesson

Ethics and law: connected, not identical

Ethics: what should we do?

Ethical design considers fairness, transparency, user choice, and avoidable harm. Ask whether data is genuinely needed, whether people understand its use, and whether a less intrusive design could work.

Example: request location while navigation is being used, explain why, and make controls easy to find.

Law: what is required?

Jurisdiction: United States, federal law. Section 5 of the Federal Trade Commission Act prohibits unfair or deceptive acts or practices in or affecting commerce. Misleading privacy promises or unfair data handling can raise legal issues; whether a practice violates law depends on the facts and applicable rules.

COPPA applies to certain online services collecting personal information from children under 13; it is not a universal privacy law.
